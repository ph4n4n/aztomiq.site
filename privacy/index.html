Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12/23/2025

1. Data Collection

At AZtomiq, your privacy is our top priority. We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data you enter into the tools.

  • Calculation data for tax, salary, BMI... is only processed on your browser.
  • Web Playground: Any code you write and run is executed locally (client-side). Our servers do not store your source code.
  • When you close the tab or reload the page, temporary data disappears (unless you choose to save it to LocalStorage History).

2. Local Storage & Cookies

We use Local Storage for the following purposes:

  • Saving theme preferences (Dark/Light mode).
  • Saving calculation history (if enabled). This data remains entirely on your device.

We do not use Cookies for tracking personal behavior.

3. Third-party Services

The website may use anonymous analytics to improve performance. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page.
